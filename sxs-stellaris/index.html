Client-data limit & pricing note

The v85 client configuration contains 75 Stellaris tiers and ends at 15,000,000 USD points, approximately $150,000. It contains no higher configured ladder tier. Concierge, manually granted, private, or server-side VIP rewards—if offered above that amount—would not appear in this client table. Prices remain approximate because of platform pricing, tax, exchange-rate adjustments, and regional pack availability. This tool does not recommend spending.
